Odolo Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

Odolo ("the app", "we") is a GPS speedometer and trip odometer for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. We designed the app to collect as little as possible: there is no account, no sign-up, and your trips are stored only on your device.

1. Information the app uses

  • Precise location (GPS). The app reads your device's location while you use it, only to compute your speed, distance, duration, top speed and average speed. This data is processed on your device. We do not send your location to our servers, and we do not operate servers that receive it.
  • Trip history. When you save a trip, its summary (date, distance, duration, speeds) is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded anywhere. You can delete it inside the app or by uninstalling the app or clearing its storage.
  • Preferences. Settings such as theme, language, units and speed limit are stored locally on your device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of the app shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device and usage information, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device model and operating system version, and approximate location derived from your IP address, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized advertising is used, and you can change your choice later where the app offers that option, or through your device settings.

Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

3. Purchases (Google Play and RevenueCat)

If you buy a subscription or upgrade, the payment is handled by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases and manage your entitlement. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase information (such as which product you bought and its status). We never receive or store your payment card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not share your location or trip history with anyone.
  • We do not require you to create an account.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children. The app is not directed to children under 13.

5. Your choices and control

  • Location: you can deny or revoke the location permission at any time in your Android settings. Without it, the speedometer cannot work.
  • Ads: change your consent choice where the app offers it, or reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Your data: because trips and settings are stored only on your device, uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

6. Security

Data stored by the app stays on your device and is protected by your device's own security. Third-party services (Google AdMob, Google Play, RevenueCat) apply their own security measures.

7. Safe use

Do not operate the app while driving. Speed readings come from GPS and may be inaccurate, so they should not be relied on as a substitute for your vehicle's instruments or for traffic laws and speed limits.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy: atlas8495@gmail.com
